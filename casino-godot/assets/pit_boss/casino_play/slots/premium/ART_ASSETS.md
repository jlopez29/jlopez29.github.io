# Premium slots raster artwork

Production PNG artwork uses the renderer's normalized cabinet coordinates.
Cabinet: landscape 1080x800; portrait 780x1000. Reel aperture: x=10%, y=23%, width=80%, height=48%. Reel dividers/metal bezel live in transparent glass overlays. Symbols are isolated 512x512 RGBA, centered; reels are dynamically drawn and clipped by Godot. FX sprites have transparent backgrounds. All text and payouts stay dynamic; the art has no baked numbers or buttons. The alternate icons are optional future tier designs.

Source artwork was derived from the project's generated concept asset sheet and glossy symbol atlas; do not substitute their concept images directly for these separated game assets.
