HOW TO PLAY

Keep the word moving.

  1. Replace one letter by selecting it, then choosing a new letter.
  2. Add one letter by selecting a plus sign between letters.
  3. Remove one letter by selecting it and using the remove button.

Every move must make a real, unused word. A valid move scores one point and resets the 30-second clock.
